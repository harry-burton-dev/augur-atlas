A thing's page now leads one step on to everything known of it: what is known now, what it is linked to both ways, and what was true before, each line with when it was said and on how many reports. Its actions work. "Keep an eye on it" is kept in Data Atlas's own table, per floor, and the page says so. "Something's wrong" picks a line, takes a reason and disputes it in the knowledge graph as the person (`knowledge:act`), once per press. "Ask about it" and "Send this" stay on the page and say why they aren't here yet.

The Backstage catalog now lists the knowledge API among what the service calls (app tools 0.6.0).

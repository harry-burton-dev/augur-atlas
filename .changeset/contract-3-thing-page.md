Data Atlas starts again on the app contract (contract 3). It reads the knowledge graph as the person, through the gateway (`knowledge:read`), and shows each thing in the kit's one shape: what it is, the answer (Now, Next, What or When), what's wrong, key facts and what is around it, each line with whose report and when. A find box opens things by name. The SDK 1 and 2 app it replaces, with its own facts database, is gone: Data Atlas keeps none of the graph.

Details can hold more (PER-328). A **quantity** is a number in a unit, such as a gross tonnage in t. A **choice** is one of a few values, such as a vessel's status. A **place** is written as words for now. The studio asks a quantity for its unit and a choice for its values, and the entity form offers a choice's values to pick from. Quantities show with their unit.

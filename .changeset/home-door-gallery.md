Data Atlas opens on a door gallery: "What do you need?" on top finds a thing, and below it four ways in. "See what has changed" lights up when something you keep an eye on has new lines, "Put something right" when a thing on your floor has two answers at once, and "Keep an eye on something" lists what you follow. "Get ready for a meeting" stays and says AUGUR has no calendar yet. The shell lights each door from what Data Atlas read; the doors are configuration in `service/src/home.ts`, so adding one is adding it there. Finding a thing moves to `/find`.

What is around a thing is now ranked by use too (Data Atlas PDR-002). When someone opens a thing from another thing's page, Data Atlas counts the link they followed, for that kind of thing on that floor. It keeps only counts, never who. Once things have been opened five times from a kind's pages, a link that led to them rises, up to three times its cold-start rank when it led to every one. Until then the cold-start ranking holds.

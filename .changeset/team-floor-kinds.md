Kinds and details can be kept for one team's floor. In the studio, an admin standing on a team's floor chooses **Kept for**: the whole organisation, or that team and the teams under it. A team's kind shows only from that team down, and nobody records one where it doesn't show. **Make it the organisation's** widens it, the only way it changes floor. Migration `016_team_terms`.
